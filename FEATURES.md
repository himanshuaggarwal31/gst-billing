## Why Switch to GST Billing?

Most small businesses in India still use Excel for invoicing — manually calculating GST, copy-pasting client details, losing track of who's paid. GST Billing eliminates all of that.

---

## Core Features (Available on Free Plan)

### 📄 GST-Compliant Tax Invoices
Generate professional Tax Invoices in seconds. GST is calculated automatically — CGST + SGST for intra-state, IGST for inter-state. You'll never miscalculate again.

### 📦 Products & Services Catalog
Save your commonly billed items — name, HSN/SAC code, rate, GST%. When creating an invoice, pick from your catalog and the row fills itself. One click instead of retyping every time.

### 🧾 PDF Download
Download a pixel-perfect, A4-formatted Tax Invoice PDF instantly. Suitable for printing or attaching to emails.

### 📧 Email Invoice
Send the invoice directly to your client's inbox with one click. The PDF is attached automatically. No more downloading and re-attaching.

### 👥 Client Management
Store client details (name, GSTIN, address, state) once. Pick the client when creating an invoice — all fields auto-fill.

### 📊 Dashboard
See your total billed amount, GST collected, and outstanding invoices at a glance. Know your business health without opening a spreadsheet.

### 🔢 Smart Invoice Numbering
Invoice numbers are auto-suggested (INV-001 → INV-002 → …) and follow your existing pattern. Fully editable if you prefer custom sequences.

## Advanced Features (Pro Plan)

### � GSTR-1 Export
One-click CSV export of all invoices in GSTR-1 compatible format (B2B and B2C sections). Choose any month, download instantly, and hand it to your accountant or upload directly to the GST portal.
### 📋 GSTR-3B Export
Download a ready-to-file GSTR-3B summary CSV for any month — outward supplies, ITC from expenses, and net GST payable — in one click. Covers Table 3.1 (output tax) and Table 4A (ITC). Works alongside GSTR-1 for complete monthly return filing.

### 🧮 ITC Ledger (Input Tax Credit)
See exactly how much GST you can claim back from your business expenses each month. The ITC Ledger shows GST collected on invoices, GST paid on purchases, and your net monthly liability — so you never overpay.

### 🔢 HSN/SAC Code Autocomplete
Start typing a product code or description and the invoice form instantly suggests matching HSN (goods) and SAC (services) codes with their standard GST rates. Covers 80+ common codes across IT services, manufacturing, trading, and more.
### 📈 Business Analytics
See your revenue, expenses, and GST in interactive charts. Track monthly trends, identify your top clients by revenue, and know your net GST liability at a glance — without touching a spreadsheet.

### �🔄 Recurring Invoices
Set up a recurring template for your retainer clients. The app auto-generates and sends the invoice on the 1st of every month. Set once, never touch again.

### 🧾 Credit Notes
Issue a credit note against any invoice for returns or corrections. Fully GST-compliant, linked to the original invoice.

### 💸 Expense Tracking
Log your business purchases with GST paid. The app calculates your net GST liability (GST collected − GST paid on purchases) — exactly what your CA needs each quarter.

### � Payment Tracking
Record partial or full payments against any invoice — with payment date, method (bank transfer, UPI, cheque, cash, card), and reference number. The invoice status updates automatically to Pending, Partial, or Paid. Full transaction history per invoice, with the ability to remove incorrect entries.

### 👨‍💼 Accountant / CA Access
Invite your chartered accountant with read-only access to your account. They can view all invoices and expenses without you sending files every month.
